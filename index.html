    5. Cyber Safety Knowledge Base

    Search through foundational rules for staying secure across web browsers, email inboxes, and mobile devices.

    🔐 Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

    Always turn on MFA on vital accounts. Even if a scammer steals your password, they cannot log in without the second verification step.

    🏦 Verify Banking Requests Directly

    If you receive a phone call or text claiming your bank account is compromised, hang up and call the number printed on your actual card.

    📶 Avoid Sensitive Tasks on Public Wi-Fi

    Unencrypted public networks can allow attacker interception. Use cellular data or a trusted VPN when logging into sensitive portals.

    🔄 Keep Devices and Software Updated

    System updates patch security vulnerabilities. Enable automatic updates for your browser, operating system, and security apps.
